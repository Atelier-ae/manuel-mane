The Artist

Art without filters.

Manuel Mané creates expressive contemporary paintings driven by bold colours, raw linework and uncompromising energy. His work moves between street art, pop culture and neo-expressionism.

Every painting is an original work, created to bring character and a strong visual presence into a space.
